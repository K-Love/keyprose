The service

KeyProse is a set of pages and an API that draft real estate copy from what you type.

  • Listing Writer drafts an MLS-style description.
  • CMA Assistant calculates a value range from the comparable sales you enter, writes a summary, and returns a PDF.
  • Social Generator drafts posts for Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X, or LinkedIn.
  • Email Campaigns drafts an email sequence.
  • Outreach Engine shows a preview of a sequence.

There is no account. There is no sign-in. Using a tool does not create an account and does not buy anything.

The email tool does not send email. The outreach tool does not email the contacts you type. The social tool does not post to Instagram, Facebook, X, or LinkedIn. The site does not connect to an MLS or a CRM. If you publish, send, or upload a draft, you do that in some other product.

Sample copy is used when a live draft is not available, is limited, is too long, or fails. Live drafts on the public site are sent to Abacus RouteLLM at routellm.abacus.ai. That was the host on November 7, 2026. The Privacy Policy says what that does and does not mean.

The CMA is not an appraisal. The numbers come from the comparable sales you enter. The written summary can be sample text or text from the model. The PDF says it is not an appraisal. A valuation-style summary does not make me, or KeyProse, a licensed appraiser.
